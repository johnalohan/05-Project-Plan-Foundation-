## Scope
### In Scope, Phase One
[Patient intake workflow standardization across all six clinics, Appointment scheduling system replacement, Staff training for the new system at all six location, Initial rollout support for 90 days post-launch]

### Out of Scope, Phase One
[Patient-facing online scheduling portal, Billing system integration with the new scheduling data, Executive reporting dashboard for clinic leadership, Mobile application for clinic staff]

### Scope Justification
[I marked Patient facing online scheduling portal because it made the project more complex and it requires more security. 
Including the model will require interface testing and data privacy concerns. 
If we push this idea out of scope, we can protect the project's time and cost. 
The team will continue to focus more on training the internal scheduling system replacement first.]

## Planning Constraints
### Constraint 1
[The new scheduling system must follow the healthcare data privacy laws and security regulations for handling a patient's personal information.]
**Effect on Planning:** [This constraint affects the quality and the cost because the system must included solid encryption. This will overall increase the budget.]

### Constraint 2
[The clinic staff and clinic managers will have a very limited availability for training because of their patient-care responsibilities. ]
**Effect on Planning:** [The constraint affects the schedule and resources because the training must be delivered before the system rolls out. This might extend the deployment time of the scheduling system.]

## Assumptions
[Assumption 1: We are assuming that the clinic's current network hardware can handle the new digital scheduling dashboard without lagging.
If this is wrong, then we would have to adjust our project cost and schedule to upgrade the physical RCHN clinic's infrastructure before deploying the new system.

Assumption 2: We are assuming that the clinic staff possesses the digital knowledge to integrate with the new platform with little to no standard training. If this is wrong, then the project schedule will have to extend and we might have to increase our budget for 1 on 1 hand on training.]
